Privacy Policy for DORNNY LIVE

Last updated: September 12, 2026

DORNNY LIVE is a live shopping platform.

1. Camera and Microphone

We use camera/mic only when seller taps "Start Live Video". The video is streamed directly to buyers via PeerJS (P2P). We do NOT record or store your live video on our servers.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect personal data. Products, cart, and messages are stored locally on your device (localStorage). Login is local only.

3. WhatsApp

Checkout uses WhatsApp link. Your phone opens WhatsApp with order details. We don't access your WhatsApp.

4. Third Party

We use PeerJS Cloud for live streaming connection (P2P). No data sold.
